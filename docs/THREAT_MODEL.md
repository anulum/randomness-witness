# Data and authority boundaries

## Present tooling

The maintained checkout and selected development environment are execution inputs for repository controls. Received descriptors, dependency receipts and generated inventories cannot nominate acceptance policy or executables. Checks inspect bounded local scopes and report fixed diagnostics. Source byte identity is separate from source behaviour, statistical suitability, current trust and hosted execution.

An inventory is not a concurrent filesystem snapshot or power-failure durability guarantee. Native child processes use maintained finite argument vectors and direct-child deadlines; completed-output limits are not live volume ceilings. Foundation qualification is in progress.

## Future source and observation inputs

Plans, streams, capture maps, source records, numerical profiles and certificates are supplied data. They do not grant acquisition, program execution, signing, provider submission, spend, publication or device authority. Independently configured source and numerical methods must precede interpreting those objects.

Literal validation precedes narrowing conversion. Full meaningful lengths, packing, padding, per-stream order and failed or missing populations remain visible. No silent cropping, cross-stream pairing, histogram-derived order or unavailable-to-zero conversion is admissible. Processing preserves raw failures and its own complete index map; the prior profile fixes odd-tail policy before observation.

Passing batteries, low pairwise correlation and chosen seeds cannot supply independent sampling or a computation graph's assumptions. Missing sample, source, clock, numerical or current-trust admission remains unavailable. Source suitability and evidence eligibility remain separate. Certification, provider execution and operational recovery are unimplemented domain boundaries; no isolation, scientific, physical or durability guarantee is claimed here.
