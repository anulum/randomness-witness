# Decision: separate source qualification from arithmetic checking and acquisition governance

## Context

A source name, matching seed, low discrepancy label or passing descriptive battery cannot establish a stochastic computation's whole joint law. Processing can also change stream lengths, alignment and provenance while leaving raw failures unresolved.

## Decision

Randomness Witness owns source-specific capture consumption, processing, batteries and scoped source/rate/use certificates. SC-NeuroCore retains actual generator implementations and counterparts; Loop Timing Witness owns capture and timebase. SC Stream Prover independently checks arithmetic assumptions. Shared evidence operations come from installed Evidence Manifest Toolkit; Experiment Ledger retains generic storage and provider-spend governance.

## Consequences

Repository tooling can be qualified before source acquisition or batteries exist. It cannot qualify absent source laws or clocks. Complete raw and processed populations, exact maps, independent references, genuine method applicability and actual consumer behaviour are required for domain support. The separate QPU/QRNG adjunct supplies ordering provenance without granting provider, physical or universal randomness authority.
